HyperMango

A free, browser-based multimedia stack authoring tool.

Hosted at: mango.yebooyah.club

What it is: HyperMango lets you build interactive, multi-card "stacks" — combining drawings, images, buttons, and simple scripts — entirely in your web browser. Think of it as a modern, browser-based take on classic stack-based authoring tools. No installation, no account required to try it.

What HyperMango Does

HyperMango is a static, client-side web app (no backend server) for creating and editing multi-card "stacks." Within the app you can:

  • Create new cards and arrange them into a stack.
  • Draw and place images directly on a card's canvas.
  • Add interactive buttons that link cards together or run simple scripts.
  • Save your work as a local .hweb file, or to your own Google Drive.
  • Open and resume editing any .hweb stack you've previously created.

HyperMango is designed for personal projects, hobby interactive fiction, and educational use, including K–12 classrooms via Google Classroom and Google Drive.

Google Account Access

HyperMango works entirely offline in your browser by default. If you choose to connect your Google account, the app requests a single, narrow OAuth scope — https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file — which only allows it to read and save the specific files you open or create with HyperMango. It cannot see, list, or modify any other file in your Drive. We do not use this access for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond opening and saving your own stack files.

Full details on what data is accessed, why, and how it's handled are in our Privacy Policy.
